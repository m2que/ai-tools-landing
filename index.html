Features

Focused tools for sharper marketing diagnostics

MarketLearn tools help you move from description to diagnosis. One evaluates the drivers of brand equity using Keller's framework, while the other tests whether a brand's intended positioning aligns with likely consumer associations.

How to utilize the toolkit

Our interactive tools deliver structured analyses with transparent reasoning, meaningful comparisons, and practical outputs for coursework, presentations, case discussions, and managerial decision-making.

  • Analyze all aspects of Keller's brand equity model, with clear explanations of the factors driving each score, to identify where brand equity is strong and where it may be vulnerable.
  • Compare stated brand positioning against likely customer perceptions, competitors, and find gaps in the market.
  • Use visual outputs to support presentations, tutorials, and class discussion.
  • Integrate verified, linked references into reports and proposals to support stronger analysis and more credible recommendations.
